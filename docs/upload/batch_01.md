## How to schedule each one (YouTube Studio)

**On a computer (studio.youtube.com):**
1. Click **Create**, then **Upload videos**, and pick the `.mp4`.
2. **Details:** paste the Title and Description. Click **Show more**, then paste the Tags. Set Category to **Film & Animation**.
3. **Thumbnail:** upload the matching `.jpg` if YouTube offers an upload button for Shorts on your account. If it doesn't, pick the best frame.
4. **Audience:** choose **No, it's not made for kids**. Under Altered content, choose **Yes**.
5. **Visibility:** choose **Schedule**, then set the date and time from the table above. Click **Schedule**.

**On the YouTube phone app:**
1. Tap **+**, then **Upload a video**, and pick the file. YouTube makes it a Short automatically because it's vertical and under 60 seconds.
2. Tap **Next**, add the title, and tap the thumbnail to choose a cover.
3. Under **Visibility**, choose **Schedule** and set the time.
4. Under **Select audience**, choose **No, it's not made for kids**. Then tap **Upload Short**.

After each Short goes live, pin the comment straight away and reply to the first comments. Early replies help the video get pushed to more viewers.
